## Description

Feeling the weight of rising prices, bills, or debt? The 30-Day Stewardship Reset is a faith-filled money workbook that walks you through one small, practical step a day, with scripture to keep your heart anchored while you do the work.

Each day takes about 15 minutes: a King James verse, a short devotional, and one clear task. Some days you'll write. Some days you'll make a phone call with our word-for-word scripts. Every day you'll move a little closer to peace with money.
