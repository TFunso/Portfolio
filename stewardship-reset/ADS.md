## 3 short videos to film (15–30 sec, phone camera, no face needed)

**Video 1: Flip-through ("What's inside")**
- 0–3s: Hands open the printed cover. Text on screen: "If money stress is keeping you up at night…"
- 3–15s: Flip slowly: Day 1 → scripts page → debt tracker → $1,000 circles. Text: "30 days · 15 minutes a day · KJV scripture"
- 15–20s: Color in 3 circles on the emergency fund page. Text: "Every circle is $10 saved."
- End card: ad-01 image. "Link in bio."

**Video 2: "Say this to your internet provider"**
- 0–3s: Close-up of Script A. Text: "Say this to your internet provider 👇"
- 3–20s: Read the four lines of Script A out loud, or show them as captions.
- 20–25s: Text: "6 scripts like this inside the 30-Day Stewardship Reset."

**Video 3: "Day 1 with me"**
- 0–3s: Coffee, Bible, printed Day 1 page. Text: "Starting a 30-day money reset with God."
- 3–20s: Read John 15:5, then write "My why" on the page (blur personal details).
- 20–25s: Text: "Follow along for Day 2." This turns into a 30-day content series.

## Starter budget (suggested test)
- Etsy Ads: $3–5/day for 14 days (Etsy chooses the placement; your first listing image matters most).
- Pinterest: 3–5 organic pins a week using different listing images. Pinterest drives free traffic for printables.
- Meta: $5/day per angle for 5 days, then keep the angle with the lowest cost per click.
