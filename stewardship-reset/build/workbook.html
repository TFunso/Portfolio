Welcome

Before you begin

Dear friend,

If money has felt heavy lately, you are not alone. Prices keep climbing, bills arrive faster than paychecks, and debt can feel like a weight you carry everywhere. Many of us were never taught how to handle money, and even fewer were taught to handle it with God.

This workbook is not a lecture and it is not a get-rich plan. It is thirty days of small, faithful steps. Each day takes about fifteen minutes: a short scripture, a few honest sentences, and one practical task. Some days you will write. Some days you will make a phone call. Every day you will move a little closer to peace.

You will see clearly where your money goes, lower the bills you can, make a real plan for your debt, start an emergency fund, and build a budget that honors God first. Along the way, you will keep a running total of the money you find and free up.

Go at your own pace. If you miss a day, simply pick up where you left off. Grace is built into this plan.

Abiding with you,

Day16

Choose your method

Through wisdom is an house builded; and by understanding it is established.

Proverbs 24:3 (KJV)

There are two proven ways to pay off debt. Both work. The best one is the one you will actually stick with.

Today's 15-minute step
  1. Read the two methods below.
  2. Circle the one that fits you.
  3. Re-list your debts in that order.
○ Debt Snowball

Pay minimums on everything. Put every extra dollar on the smallest balance first. When it's gone, roll that payment into the next smallest.

Best if you need quick wins to stay motivated.

○ Debt Avalanche

Pay minimums on everything. Put every extra dollar on the highest interest rate first. When it's gone, roll that payment into the next highest.

Best if you want to pay the least interest overall.

Bill Negotiation Scripts

Read the script once, fill in the blanks, then pray and dial. Be calm, kind, and specific. It's fine to say, “Let me think about it,” and call back.

AInternet & Cable
You

Hi, I've been a customer for ____ years. My bill is now $____ a month and that's more than I can keep paying. Before I look at switching, I wanted to see what you can do.

If they offer little

I understand. I've seen ________ offering $____ a month for similar speed. Could you transfer me to your retention or loyalty department?

Retention

I'd like to stay, but I need my bill closer to $____. What promotions do you have for existing customers?

Close

Thank you. Could you confirm the new monthly price, how long it lasts, and email me a confirmation?

BMobile Phone
You

Hi, I'd like to review my plan. I'm paying $____ a month and only using about ____ GB of data. What's the lowest plan that fits how I actually use my phone?

Ask

Are there any loyalty, autopay, or paperless discounts I'm not getting?

If needed

________ offers a similar plan for $____. I'd rather stay with you if you can come closer to that.

Close

Great. Will this change affect my device payments or contract? Please confirm the new total.

CInsurance
You

Hi, my policy renews soon. I received a quote for $____ from ________ for the same coverage. Can you review my policy for any discounts?

Ask about

Bundling, safe driver, good student, paperless, autopay, paying in full, or a higher deductible.

If needed

Can you match or come close to $____? I'd prefer to stay if the price works.

Remember

Compare the same coverage. Never cancel your old policy until the new one is confirmed active.

Scripts, continued

DCredit Card Interest Rate
You

Hi, I've been a customer since ____ and I'm working hard to pay down my balance. I'd like to request a lower interest rate on my account.

If no

I understand. Are there any hardship programs or temporary rate reductions available?

If still no

Thank you. When can I request a review again? (Write that date in your Day 12 log.)

Remember

Be polite and brief. Ask about any effect on your credit before agreeing to a program.

EMedical Bill
You

Hi, I received a bill for $____ for a visit on ________. Before I pay, could you send me an itemized bill?

Ask

Do you offer a discount for paying in full, a financial assistance program, or a no-interest payment plan?

If needed

I can pay $____ a month. Can we set up a plan at that amount with no interest?

Remember

Check the itemized bill for duplicate or incorrect charges. Get every agreement in writing.

FCancelling a Subscription
You

Hi, I'd like to cancel my subscription, effective today.

If they offer a deal

Thank you. Is that discount permanent or temporary? (Only accept if you truly want to keep it.)

Close

Please confirm the cancellation and that I won't be charged again. Can you email me a confirmation?

Remember

Note the confirmation number and check next month's statement.
