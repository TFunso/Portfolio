# Five NGX Stocks Worth Owning (1–3 Year View)

*Screen date: 24 September 2026 · Market: Nigerian Exchange (NGX)*

No one can promise a stock will do well. These are the five NGX names where quality, price and dividends currently line up best. This is a screen, not a full 15-step analysis.

| # | Stock | Price (approx.) | Why it's on the list | Main risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **GTCO** | ~₦140 (May; current not verified) | Highest-quality big bank. ROE ~25%, P/E ~5.7x, FY2025 dividend ₦12.76 (~9% yield at ₦140). | Rate cuts squeeze margins; priced above book (1.35x). |
| 2 | **Zenith Bank** | ₦128.40 | P/E ~5x at ~book value, ROE ~25%, ~₦10/yr dividends (~7.8% yield). Added to FTSE Frontier 50 in September. | Q1 2026 profit flat (+0.7%); earnings growth stalling. |
| 3 | **MTN Nigeria** | ₦890 | H1 2026 profit +71% to ₦707.5bn; data revenue +38%; net cash. Interim dividend ₦26. P/E ~13x annualised. | Already re-rated; tariff and regulatory politics. |
| 4 | **Seplat Energy** | ₦14,908 (single source; verify) | Earns and pays in USD. H1 EBITDA ~$1bn (52% margin). Expected 2026 dividend 68.3¢/share (~6% yield). | Oil-price exposure; P/E ~20x on NGX price looks rich (cheaper on EBITDA). |
| 5 | **Dangote Cement** | ~₦1,060 | Dominant market position. H1 profit ₦638.5bn = 63% of FY2025. Dividend +50% to ₦45 (~4.3% yield). P/E ~14x annualised. | Up 77% in a year; energy costs. |

## If funding only one or two

Start with **Zenith and GTCO** for income and valuation, add **MTN Nigeria** for growth. **Seplat** matters most if you think in dollars.

## Deliberately excluded

- **Access Holdings** — cheap, but dividends blocked by the regulator. Rated Hold. See the Access report.
- **Dangote Refinery IPO** — overpriced at ₦525. Rated Watchlist. See the Dangote report.

## Three cautions

1. **Concentration.** All five are Nigeria; three are banks or financial-type businesses. One naira or sovereign shock hits all of them. If investing from dollars, cap Nigeria at a portion of the portfolio.
2. **Naira risk.** A 20% naira gain can shrink a lot in dollars. 7–9% yields only help if the naira holds.
3. **Data quality.** Figures come from news and data sites; some prices are weeks old. Check live prices with a broker before buying.
