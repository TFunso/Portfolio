How much do I need to replace my salary?

Formula: target × 12 ÷ (yield × (1 − tax withheld)). A 4% yield from dividends that grow every year is usually safer than a 10%+ yield that may be cut.

How to build it

  1. Start with two monthly payers (for example Realty Income and Main Street Capital). That alone puts money in your account every month.
  2. Add one quarterly payer from each cycle: Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct, Feb/May/Aug/Nov, and Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec. The planner's "12-month ladder" preset shows this.
  3. Favour growing dividends over high yields. Very high yields (PFLT ~15%, some telecoms ~6%) often mean the market expects a cut.
  4. Mind the ex-dividend date. Buy before it to receive the next payment.
  5. Reinvest until you retire (most brokers offer automatic dividend reinvestment, or DRIP), then switch to taking the cash.
  6. Spread across sectors. REITs and BDCs pay the most but are sensitive to interest rates and credit. Balance them with healthcare, consumer staples and energy.
